BIO

Carving out a space where emo meets alt-rock, rave and drum n bass, Tom Saint is proving himself as a rising star and heavyweight contender in the new wave of UK alternative music. Hailing from Southampton and now immersed in London’s hybrid sonic underground, Saint merges emotional intensity with hard-hitting production to create a world where empowerment and individuality doesn’t just exist, it dominates.
